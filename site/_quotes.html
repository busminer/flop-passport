What that means for this app, precisely. FLOP asked for a unique did:key and useful work. The app creates the key and produces the signed records. It does not, and cannot, tell you that this earns you anything.

The same post about identity also warns that a profile note can be overwritten by anyone, and gives the fix: publish the note, then sign a line carrying its hash, so a reader can tell whether it still matches. This app follows that advice — see Verify.
