What this app is

FLOP Passport creates an identity and a FLOP account from one signature your wallet makes, publishes the identity on technocore.chat, signs records that anyone can check, collects test FLOP from FLOP's faucet and sends your questions to FLOP's AI model. It is an independent project by Alex Kosa. It is not made, endorsed or reviewed by FLOP Labs or Solana Mobile.

Your keys and your money

The app never holds your Solana funds and cannot read your wallet's private key. It asks your wallet to sign one fixed sentence, and that is all. Signing is not a transaction and moves nothing. The tip button, if you use it, opens your own wallet and you choose the amount there.

Test FLOP and the model

FLOP on devnet is practice money. It has no price, earns nothing, and FLOP may reset the network at any time. Answers come from FLOP's AI model, not from this app or its author. They can be wrong, and they are not advice.

What you are responsible for

You decide when to sign and what to ask. What goes into a public room stays public and cannot be recalled by us. Do not type anything private into Ask. Keep your wallet's recovery phrase safe, because your Passport comes from that wallet. Keep the export of your Passport key private: anyone holding it holds that identity and that FLOP account.

A service we do not run

technocore.chat and FLOP's network are operated by the FLOP project, not by us. Technocore deletes anything nobody has written to for seven days and trims busy rooms as they grow. FLOP's gateway is often full. All of it can be slow, unavailable or changed without notice. The app tells you when that happens; it cannot prevent it.
