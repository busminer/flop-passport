Privacy policy

FLOP Passport for Seeker

Last updated 31 August 2026. This policy covers the Android application dev.alexkosa.flooppassport and this website.

The short version. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server operated by us. The app does not collect, transmit or store your personal data anywhere we can reach. Your key stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

FLOP Passport for Seeker is an independent open-source project by Alex Kosa, an individual developer. It is not made, endorsed or reviewed by FLOP Labs or Solana Mobile. Contact: @AlexKosa1 on X, or by opening an issue in the public repository.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your Passport key, encrypted. It is stored only as ciphertext, wrapped by a key held in your phone's hardware-backed key store, which cannot be read out of it.
  • Your public identity — the did:key and its fingerprint — and the Solana wallet address it was derived from.
  • Your language preference and whether the launch sound is enabled.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Android's automatic backup and device-transfer are switched off for this app deliberately, so none of it is copied to a cloud backup.

What leaves your device, and only when you press a button

  • To technocore.chat, when you publish your identity or sign a record: your public did:key, the text you are signing, a counter and the signature. This information is public by design — it is the point of a public identity — and anyone can read it afterwards.
  • To your wallet app, when you create the Passport: a statement to sign. The wallet handles this on your device; nothing is sent over the network by us.
  • To a file you choose, when you export your key. You pick the destination through the system file picker. Nothing is uploaded.

Nothing is ever sent to the developer. There is no backend to send it to. The app talks to technocore.chat, operated by FLOP Labs, and to your own wallet app — and that is the complete list.

What the app never asks for and never touches

  • Your Solana wallet's recovery phrase or private key. The app has never seen it and cannot.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no ad SDKs.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking of any kind. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no server-side code; the only outbound request it makes is for the two web fonts and, when you use the verifier, a direct read from technocore.chat that goes from your browser and never through us.

Permissions

  • Internet — to reach technocore.chat when you publish or sign something.
  • Vibrate — a short confirmation tick when a step is verified.
  • Biometric — to gate the screen that shows your private key.

No storage permission is requested; saving a file uses the system picker instead.

Deleting your data

On your device. Settings → Danger zone → delete the Passport, or uninstall the app. Both remove the encrypted key, your identity and your preferences from the phone completely. There is no copy anywhere else, and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

What was published. Anything you chose to publish on Technocore is public and outside our control — we do not host it and cannot remove it. Records in public rooms are dropped by that service as rooms are compacted; notes persist. Requests about content on technocore.chat go to FLOP Labs, who operate it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the previous version remains in the public repository's history, where anyone can compare them.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, portability and erasure exist to give you control over data somebody else holds about you. We hold none: there is nothing to access, export or erase, because nothing about you ever reaches us. Your key is exportable from the app at any time, and deleting the app removes everything it kept.
