Privacy policy

FLOP Passport for Seeker

Last updated 6 November 2026. This policy covers the Android application dev.alexkosa.floppassport and this website.

The short version. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server operated by us. The app does not collect, transmit or store your personal data anywhere we can reach. Your key stays on your phone. What you type into Ask goes to FLOP's AI model, so do not type anything private there.

Who is responsible

FLOP Passport for Seeker is an independent open-source project by Alex Kosa, an individual developer. It is not made, endorsed or reviewed by FLOP Labs or Solana Mobile. Contact: flop-passport@alexkosa.dev, or @AlexKosa1 on X, or by opening an issue in the public repository.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your Passport key, encrypted. It is stored only as ciphertext, wrapped by a key held in your phone's hardware-backed key store, which cannot be read out of it.
  • Your public identity — the did:key and its fingerprint — and the Solana wallet address it was derived from.
  • Your FLOP activity: your questions and the model's answers, the key FLOP's gateway issued to your account, your balance, your streak and your signed days.
  • A post line or post link, only if you saved one in an earlier version of the app. The link names your social account, and it stays in your public identity note until you delete the Passport.
  • Your language preference, when your Passport was created and which security level sealed it, when your note was last written to, and which reminders have already been shown.

All of it lives in the app's private storage on your device. Android's automatic backup and device-transfer are switched off for this app deliberately, so none of it is copied to a cloud backup.

What leaves your device

  • To technocore.chat, when you publish your identity or sign a record: your public did:key, the text you are signing, a counter and the signature. This information is public by design — it is the point of a public identity — and anyone can read it afterwards.
  • To FLOP's faucet, when you collect test FLOP: your FLOP address.
  • To FLOP's gateway at api.flop.network, when you use Ask or Do My Day: your FLOP address, a signature made by your Passport, and every question you type. FLOP operates that service and its AI model, and this app has no control over what they do with what they receive.
  • To FLOP's devnet node and explorer: your FLOP address, to read your balance and what you have spent.
  • To flop.alexkosa.dev: a plain request for two public files, the status and the news shown in the app. The request carries nothing about you beyond the network address any request carries.
  • To your wallet app, when you create the Passport: a statement to sign. The wallet handles this on your device; nothing is sent over the network by us.
  • To a file you choose, when you export your key. You pick the destination through the system file picker. Nothing is uploaded.
  • To technocore.chat, without you pressing anything, every time you open the app and again when you look at your records: the app reads your note and your records back, and rewrites the note. That rewrite is the whole point — that service deletes anything nothing has written to for seven days, so opening the app is what keeps your identity from being deleted. It sends the same note that is already public there.

Nothing is ever sent to the developer. There is no backend to send it to. The app talks to technocore.chat and to FLOP's gateway, faucet, devnet node and explorer, all operated by FLOP Labs, to the static site flop.alexkosa.dev, and to your own wallet app. That is the complete list.

What the app never asks for and never touches

  • Your Solana wallet's recovery phrase or private key. The app has never seen it and cannot.
  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account credentials.
  • Your contacts, photos, files, location, camera or microphone.
  • Advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no ad SDKs.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking of any kind. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no server-side code. Its only outbound requests are for two web fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the way any font host does, and — when you use the verifier — a direct read from technocore.chat that goes from your browser and never through us. The site itself is static files served by Cloudflare, which handles the request; we run no code of our own and keep no logs.

Permissions

  • Internet — to reach technocore.chat and FLOP's network when you sign, collect test FLOP or ask the model, to read your records back and keep your note from expiring when you open the app, and to fetch the status and the news.
  • Vibrate — a short confirmation tick when a step is verified.
  • Biometric — to gate the screen that shows your private key. The older Fingerprint permission is pulled in by the Android biometric library for the same purpose.
  • Network state — used by the wallet-adapter library to tell whether the phone is online before it opens your wallet. The app itself never reads it.
  • Notifications — the daily reminder, the nudges about a step you have not finished, and the warning before your note expires.
  • Run at startup — only to set those reminders again after the phone reboots. The app does nothing else on boot.

No storage permission is requested; saving a file uses the system picker instead.

Deleting your data

On your device. Settings → Danger zone → delete the Passport removes the encrypted key, your identity, your FLOP activity, your post link and the reminder state; uninstalling the app removes your language and reminder settings too. There is no copy anywhere else, and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

What was published. Anything you chose to publish on Technocore is public and outside our control — we do not host it and cannot remove it. Records in public rooms are dropped by that service as rooms are compacted, and an identity note is deleted after seven days with nothing written to it — so what you published disappears on its own unless it is rewritten. Requests about content on technocore.chat, and about what FLOP's gateway received, go to FLOP Labs, who operate them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the previous version remains in the public repository's history, where anyone can compare them.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, portability and erasure exist to give you control over data somebody else holds about you. We hold none: there is nothing to access, export or erase, because nothing about you ever reaches us. Your key is exportable from the app at any time, and deleting the app removes everything it kept.
